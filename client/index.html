STAGE GUIDE & CONTROLS

STAGE OBJECTIVE
All 4 players must collect their own Golden Key (1 key each). Avoid the spike pit in the center. Bring all 4 keys to the Exit Door and escape together!
HOW TO SOLVE: MAP 1 - THE FOUR KEYS
1
Key 1 (Left Arch): Hop onto the Left Arch from the start platform or stack teammates to grab the first key.
2
Key 2 (Center High Arch): Stack teammates 4-high or stack the 4 pushable center boxes to reach the high key.
3
Bridge the Spikes: Watch out for the deadly spikes! Push boxes into the spike pit to create a safe bridge across.
4
Key 3 & 4: Collect Key 3 atop the Right Arch and Key 4 in the lower right chamber.
5
Escape Together: Bring all 4 keys to the Exit Door on the far right. All players must enter together to win!
CONTROLS
Player 1 (Host): A / D (Move), W / Space (Jump)
Player 2 (Local): ← / → (Move), ↑ (Jump)
Restart Stage: R (Instant Retry)
Quick Emotes: Number keys 1 to 6
Toggle Guide: H or click GUIDE (H)
COOPERATIVE TIPS
  • Stacking: Jump on a teammate's head to reach higher places.
  • High Jump: Hold down the Jump button to jump higher. A quick tap gives a small hop.
  • Chain Mode: Stay close to your partner so you don't pull each other off platforms!
